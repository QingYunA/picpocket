SECTION 01

The service服务内容

PicPocket ("we", "our") is a Chrome side-panel extension for designers and AI artists. It saves images from web pages, turns them into structured prompts with an AI model, keeps a local prompt library and generates images. By installing or using PicPocket, or by creating an account, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use it. PicPocket（以下简称“我们”）是一款为设计师和 AI 创作者打造的 Chrome 侧边栏扩展，用于从网页收藏图片、借助 AI 模型把图片拆解成结构化提示词、管理本地提示词库以及生成图片。安装或使用 PicPocket、创建账号，即表示你同意本条款及我们的隐私权政策。如果不同意，请不要使用。

Two ways to use PicPocket两种使用方式
Bring your own key: requests go straight from your browser to the AI provider you configured; you are bound by that provider's terms and billed by them. Hosted service: requests go through PicPocket's servers and are paid with credits (Section 03). 自备 Key：请求直接从浏览器发往你配置的 AI 服务商，适用该服务商的条款，费用由其收取。官方托管：请求经过 PicPocket 的服务器，用积分付费（见第 03 节）。

The extension's local features (saving, organizing and exporting your library) work without an account and without an internet connection to us. 扩展的本地功能（收藏、整理和导出你的图库）无需账号，也不依赖与我们的网络连接。

Plans & credits套餐与积分

Redeem codes issued before accounts existed remain valid until their stated expiry date. 账号功能上线前发放的兑换码，在其标注的有效期内仍然可用。
