Pricing & credits 定价与积分

PicPocket is free with your own API key, forever, with no account. If you'd rather not manage keys, the hosted service runs curated models for you and charges credits per request. Every account gets 30 free credits each month. 自带 API Key 使用 PicPocket 永久免费，也无需账号。如果不想自己管理 Key，可以用官方托管服务：我们提供精选模型，按次扣积分。每个账号每月赠送 30 积分。

  • Prices in USD价格单位：美元
  • Cancel any time, 7-day refund on first purchase随时取消，首购 7 天可退款

Plans订阅套餐

Subscriptions grant credits every month. Unused subscription credits expire at the end of the period. Yearly billing saves about 17%. 订阅套餐每月发放积分，未用完的订阅积分在周期结束时过期。按年付费约省 17%。

SECTION 03

What things cost积分怎么扣

Hosted requests deduct credits when they run. If a request fails on our side, its credits are returned automatically. Using your own API key never uses credits. 托管请求在执行时扣除积分；因我方原因失败的请求，积分会自动退回。使用自己的 API Key 不消耗积分。

Action操作 Credits积分 How it is charged计费方式
Analyze an image into a prompt图片反推提示词 1 – 8 Per request, based on the tokens actually used and the model you pick每次请求，按实际消耗的 token 和所选模型计
Generate an image生成图片 6 – 20 Per image, fixed by the model you pick每张图，由所选模型决定
Generate a 4K image生成 4K 图片 up to 32 Per image, on models that offer 4K output每张图，仅限支持 4K 输出的模型

Exact per-model credit costs are shown inside the extension under Settings → Account & Plan. Costs may change when upstream model prices change; changes never reduce credits you already hold. 各模型的具体积分消耗见扩展内“设置 → 账号与套餐”。上游模型价格变化时消耗可能调整，但不会减少你已持有的积分。

SECTION 04

Billing details计费说明

  • Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Waffo Pancake, or with PayPal. Waffo acts as merchant of record for its payments and handles applicable taxes; the price at checkout is what you pay.可通过 Waffo Pancake 使用银行卡、Apple Pay、Google Pay 付款，或使用 PayPal。经 Waffo 的付款由其作为商户主体处理并计收适用税费，结账页价格即实际支付价格。
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel in Settings → Account & Plan. Your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.订阅自动续费，直到你在“设置 → 账号与套餐”中取消；套餐保留到已付费周期结束。
  • Full refund within 7 days of your first purchase if you have used no more than 10% of its credits. See the Terms of Service for details.首次购买后 7 天内，若已使用积分不超过 10%，可全额退款，详见服务条款。
  • Billing questions: billing@picpocket.top. Refunds: refunds@picpocket.top.账单问题：billing@picpocket.top；退款：refunds@picpocket.top。
