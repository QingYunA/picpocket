Privacy Policy 隐私权政策

PicPocket is built to be local-first. The images you save, the prompts you write and your API keys are stored in your own browser. This page explains exactly what stays on your device, when the extension connects to the internet, and what it sends. PicPocket 以本地优先为原则设计。你收藏的图片、编写的提示词和 API Key 都保存在你自己的浏览器里。本页将说明哪些数据留在你的设备上、扩展在什么时候会联网，以及会发送什么内容。

  • Last updated: September 28, 2026最近更新：2026 年 9 月 28 日
  • Applies to: PicPocket v1.0 for Chrome适用范围：PicPocket Chrome 扩展 v1.0
  • Analytics: None统计分析：无

Your library stays local灵感库只存在本地

Saved images, prompts, tags and notes live in your browser's IndexedDB. We don't run a server that stores them.收藏的图片、提示词、标签和笔记保存在浏览器的 IndexedDB 中。我们没有任何存储这些内容的服务器。

Your key goes straight to your provider你的 Key 直连你选择的服务商

With your own API key, requests go directly from your browser to the endpoint you configured. The optional redeem-code quota is the one exception, explained below.使用自己的 API Key 时，请求直接从浏览器发往你配置的接口。唯一的例外是可选的兑换码托管额度，详见下文。

No tracking, no selling不追踪，不出售

No analytics or advertising SDKs, an account is optional, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.不集成任何统计或广告 SDK，账号可选不强制，也绝不出售或为广告目的共享你的数据。

SECTION 01

Overview概述

PicPocket ("we", "our" or "the extension") is a Chrome side-panel extension for designers and AI artists. It lets you save images from web pages, turn them into structured prompts with an AI model, keep a prompt library and generate images. This policy covers the extension and the optional hosted-quota service described in Section 05. PicPocket（以下简称“我们”或“本扩展”）是一款为设计师和 AI 创作者打造的 Chrome 侧边栏扩展，用于从网页收藏图片、借助 AI 模型把图片拆解成结构化提示词、管理提示词库以及生成图片。本政策适用于本扩展，以及第 05 节所述的可选托管额度服务。

Single purpose单一用途
PicPocket has one purpose: helping you collect visual inspiration and turn it into AI image prompts. We only use data to provide that feature. PicPocket 只有一个用途：帮助你收集视觉灵感并把它变成 AI 生图提示词。我们只会为了提供这项功能而使用数据。

SECTION 03

When PicPocket goes online扩展何时联网

PicPocket doesn't send anything in the background. It only connects to the internet in these cases, each started by something you do: PicPocket 不会在后台发送任何数据。只有以下几种情况会联网，而且每一种都由你的操作触发：

  • Analyzing or generating with your own key使用自己的 Key 反推或生图: the image (and any prompt text or reference image) is sent directly from your browser to the AI endpoint you configured, such as OpenAI, DeepSeek, SiliconFlow, StepFun or a custom OpenAI-compatible server. That provider handles it under its own privacy policy. PicPocket's servers are not involved.图片（以及提示词文本或参考图）会从浏览器直接发往你配置的 AI 接口，例如 OpenAI、DeepSeek、硅基流动、阶跃星辰或自定义 OpenAI 兼容服务。该服务商按其自身的隐私政策处理这些内容，PicPocket 的服务器不参与其中。
  • Signing in (optional)登录账号（可选）: when you sign in or out, PicPocket talks to our authentication service (Supabase) and to Google or GitHub if you choose them. See Section 04.登录或退出时，PicPocket 会连接我们的认证服务（Supabase），以及你选择的 Google 或 GitHub，详见第 04 节。
  • Using hosted quota with a redeem code使用兑换码托管额度: if you choose this instead of your own key, the request goes through PicPocket's server. See Section 05.如果你选择使用兑换码而不是自己的 Key，请求会经过 PicPocket 的服务器，详见第 05 节。
  • Saving an image收藏图片: PicPocket downloads the image from the website that hosts it, the same way your browser does when showing it.PicPocket 会从图片所在的网站下载该图片，和浏览器显示图片时的方式相同。
  • Syncing the prompt library同步提示词库: when you click Sync, PicPocket downloads public prompt collections from GitHub and jsDelivr. Preview images in the library load from the public sites they are hosted on.点击“同步词库”时，PicPocket 会从 GitHub 和 jsDelivr 下载公开的提示词合集。词库中的预览图从其所在的公开网站加载。
  • Connecting an AI agent (optional)连接 AI Agent（可选）: if you turn on agent collaboration, PicPocket talks to a bridge running on your own computer (127.0.0.1). You choose whether an agent such as Cursor or Claude Code may read your library, save prompts or generate images. What you let an agent read may then be sent to that agent's own AI provider.如果你开启 Agent 协作，PicPocket 只会连接运行在你本机（127.0.0.1）上的桥接程序。Cursor、Claude Code 等 Agent 能否读取灵感库、保存提示词或生图，都由你授权。你允许 Agent 读取的内容，可能会被该 Agent 发送给它自己的 AI 服务商。
SECTION 04

Account sign-in (optional)账号登录（可选）

You can use PicPocket without an account. Signing in is optional and lets you use the same account across browsers and devices, and, once paid plans launch, link your plan and credits to it. Signing in does not upload your library: saved images, prompts and settings stay on your device. 不登录也可以正常使用 PicPocket。登录是可选的，用于在不同浏览器和设备上使用同一个账号，并在付费方案上线后把会员和积分绑定到账号。登录不会上传你的灵感库：收藏的图片、提示词和设置仍然只保存在你的设备上。

How you can sign in登录方式

  • Google: PicPocket opens www.picpocket.top, where you sign in with Google Identity Services. We request only the basic openid, email and profile scopes, so Google shares your name, email address and profile picture. We never see your Google password and don't request access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google data.PicPocket 会打开 www.picpocket.top，由你通过 Google Identity Services 登录。我们只申请基础的 openid、email、profile 权限，Google 只会提供你的姓名、邮箱地址和头像。我们看不到你的 Google 密码，也不会申请访问 Gmail、云端硬盘、通讯录或其他任何 Google 数据。
  • GitHub: GitHub shares your name, username, email address and avatar.GitHub 会提供你的名称、用户名、邮箱地址和头像。
  • Email code邮箱验证码: we email you a one-time 6-digit code. No password is created or stored.我们向你的邮箱发送一次性 6 位验证码，不会创建或保存密码。

What we keep and why我们保留什么、用来做什么

  • An account record: your email address, display name, avatar URL, the sign-in method you used, that provider's user ID, and when the account was created and last signed in.账号记录：邮箱地址、显示名称、头像地址、登录方式、该登录方式下的用户 ID，以及账号创建和最近登录时间。
  • Your sign-in session is stored in Chrome's extension storage on your device, so you stay signed in until you sign out.登录会话保存在你设备上 Chrome 的扩展存储中，在你主动退出前保持登录。
  • We use this information only to sign you in and to provide PicPocket features tied to your account. We don't use it for advertising, don't sell it, and share it only with the service providers that run sign-in for us: Supabase (account database and authentication), Resend (delivering sign-in codes), and Google or GitHub when you choose them.这些信息只用于登录和提供与账号相关的 PicPocket 功能。我们不会将其用于广告、不会出售，仅由为我们提供登录服务的服务商处理：Supabase（账号数据库与认证）、Resend（发送登录验证码），以及你选择的 Google 或 GitHub。

PicPocket's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. PicPocket 对从 Google API 获取的信息的使用和传输，遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的“限制使用（Limited Use）”要求。

SECTION 05

Hosted quota (redeem codes)托管额度（兑换码）

Some creators and beta testers get a redeem code that lets them analyze and generate images without their own API key. This is optional. If you only use your own key, this section doesn't apply to you. 部分创作者和内测用户会获得兑换码，无需自己的 API Key 也能反推和生图。这是可选功能；如果你只使用自己的 Key，本节与你无关。

How it works工作方式

  1. When you redeem a code, PicPocket checks it against our database, hosted on Supabase.兑换时，PicPocket 会在我们托管于 Supabase 的数据库中核验兑换码。
  2. Each analysis or generation request is sent with your code to our server (a Supabase Edge Function). The server checks the code and deducts one use from your quota.每次反推或生图请求都会带上兑换码发送到我们的服务器（Supabase Edge Function）。服务器核验兑换码，并扣除一次额度。
  3. The server forwards the request, including the image and prompt text, to an upstream AI provider (currently SiliconFlow for image analysis and most generation, and xAI for Grok image models), and returns the result to you.服务器将请求（包括图片和提示词文本）转发给上游 AI 服务商（目前反推和大部分生图使用硅基流动，Grok 生图模型使用 xAI），并把结果返回给你。

What we keep我们保留什么

  • We keep the redeem code, its remaining quota, its expiry date and when it was last used.我们保留兑换码、剩余额度、有效期和最近使用时间。
  • We don't store your images, prompts or the AI's responses. They pass through the server in memory and aren't written to our database.我们不保存你的图片、提示词或 AI 返回的结果。它们只在服务器内存中中转，不会写入我们的数据库。
  • Supabase, our hosting provider, may keep standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for security and operations, under its own retention policy.我们的托管服务商 Supabase 可能会出于安全和运维目的保留常规请求日志（例如 IP 地址和时间），保留期限遵循其自身政策。
  • The upstream AI provider processes the content under its own privacy policy.上游 AI 服务商按其自身的隐私政策处理请求内容。

SECTION 07

What we don't do我们不做的事

  • No account required不强制注册账号: PicPocket works without signing in. If you choose to sign in, we only receive the details listed in Section 04. We never ask for your phone number and never see your Google or GitHub password.不登录也能使用 PicPocket。如果你选择登录，我们只会获得第 04 节列出的信息；我们不会索取手机号，也看不到你的 Google 或 GitHub 密码。
  • No browsing history不收集浏览记录: we don't collect the pages you visit, your searches or your cookies. The only page details PicPocket keeps are the URL and title of pages you save images from, and those stay on your device.我们不收集你访问的网页、搜索内容或 Cookie。PicPocket 唯一保留的页面信息，是你收藏图片时所在网页的地址和标题，而且只保存在你的设备上。
  • No analytics or tracking没有统计或追踪: the extension contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.扩展中没有任何统计分析、崩溃上报或广告 SDK。
  • No selling or advertising不出售，不用于广告: we never sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it for credit or lending decisions.我们绝不出售你的数据，不为广告目的共享，也不将其用于信用评估或借贷决策。
SECTION 08

Your choices & deletion你的选择与删除

  • Delete anything随时删除: remove any image, prompt, tag or folder in the side panel, one at a time or in bulk. It's deleted from your browser immediately.可在侧边栏中逐个或批量删除图片、提示词、标签或文件夹，数据会立即从浏览器中删除。
  • Export a backup导出备份: download your library as a ZIP file whenever you like.随时可以把灵感库导出为 ZIP 文件。
  • Turn features off关闭功能: disable the hover pill, and leave agent collaboration and hosted quota switched off if you don't want them.可以关闭悬停胶囊；不需要时，也可以不开启 Agent 协作和托管额度。
  • Uninstall卸载: removing the extension makes Chrome delete everything PicPocket stored in your browser.卸载扩展后，Chrome 会删除 PicPocket 在浏览器中保存的全部数据。
  • Your account账号: sign out at any time from the account menu. To delete your account and its record, email us from the address you signed in with and we will delete it within 30 days.可随时在账号菜单中退出登录。如需注销账号并删除账号记录，请用登录时使用的邮箱给我们发邮件，我们会在 30 天内删除。
  • Redeem-code records兑换码记录: email us and we'll delete the record for your code. The code will stop working.给我们发邮件，我们会删除你的兑换码记录，该兑换码随即失效。
SECTION 09

FAQ常见问题

Can the developers see the images I save?开发者能看到我收藏的图片吗？
No. Your library is stored only in your browser and we have no access to it. If you use hosted quota, the image you analyze passes through our server on its way to the AI provider, but it isn't stored or viewed. 不能。你的灵感库只保存在你的浏览器里，我们无法访问。如果你使用托管额度，被反推的图片会经过我们的服务器转发给 AI 服务商，但不会被保存或查看。
Where is my API key stored?我的 API Key 保存在哪里？
In Chrome's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer. It's only sent to the AI endpoint you configured, when you analyze or generate an image. It is never sent to PicPocket. 保存在你电脑上 Chrome 的扩展存储（chrome.storage.local）中。只有在你反推或生图时，才会发送到你配置的 AI 接口，永远不会发送给 PicPocket。
Why does Chrome say PicPocket can "read and change data on all websites"?为什么 Chrome 提示它能“读取和更改所有网站上的数据”？
That's Chrome's standard wording for extensions that work on any site. PicPocket needs it so the hover pill and snip tool work wherever you browse. It doesn't inject ads, change page content or log where you go. 这是 Chrome 对可在任意网站运行的扩展使用的标准提示。PicPocket 需要它，悬停胶囊和框选工具才能在你浏览的任何网站上使用。它不会注入广告、篡改网页内容或记录你的浏览轨迹。
